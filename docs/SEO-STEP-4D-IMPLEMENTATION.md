## 11. SEO Improvements Intentionally Declined
- **Universal Calculation Formulas:** Declined publishing generic mathematical formulas (e.g., claiming all jewellery businesses calculate making charges by gross weight or fixed touch deductions), as different workshops and showrooms operate on distinct billing conventions.
- **SaaS / Instant Download Claims:** Declined framing products as ready-made SaaS downloads or stating fixed licensing fees, preserving truthful custom software and configurable solution positioning.
- **Hyped Marketing Claims:** Declined keywords or copy claiming "100% accuracy", "zero stock loss", "guaranteed profit improvement", or "best jewellery ERP in India".
- **Fictitious Customer Quotes & Metrics:** Declined adding fake customer review widgets, adoption counts, or unsupported client rosters.
